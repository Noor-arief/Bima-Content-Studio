Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

BIMA Content Studio is a personal content-management dashboard for its operator. This notice describes the data involved in the current workflow and any optional TikTok integration that may be enabled later.

Information used

Content entered by the operator may include draft titles, storyboards, schedules, product references, media filenames, video assets, published links and performance figures. Parts of the affiliate workspace store drafts in the browser's local storage and video files in browser-managed storage. Clearing browser data may delete those local records.

If TikTok authorization is enabled, the application may receive the account identifier, profile details and other information within the specific permissions approved by TikTok and explicitly granted by the account owner. Access to publishing or TikTok Shop affiliate information is not implied by basic sign-in permission.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is used to plan and review content, support authorized account connections, manage video workflows and display information to the operator. TikTok account access is requested only through the platform's authorization process, based on the account owner's consent and applicable platform terms.

Storage and security

Browser-local records stay in the local browser profile unless deliberately exported or submitted to an enabled external service. Authorized integrations may require secure server-side handling of access tokens; no token should be exposed in the public website or repository. The operator should protect account credentials and promptly revoke suspicious connections.

Sharing and providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure or integrations selected by the operator, such as website hosting, an authorized platform API, or a chosen media-generation provider, but only when those services are actually used. BIMA does not sell personal data. Third-party services may process data according to their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Browser-stored data remains until the operator deletes it or clears browser data. Data stored in an authorized integration, if enabled, should be retained only as needed for the workflow and applicable obligations. The operator can revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok's account settings; revocation may prevent further data access but may not automatically erase existing locally saved drafts.

Audience

This application is intended for the operator's personal use and is not designed for use by children or public sign-ups.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion inquiries, contact hello@vireqo.id. This notice will be updated if the application's actual data practices change.
