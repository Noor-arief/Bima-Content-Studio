# BIMA TikTok Affiliate — Creative & Voice-over Standard

Version: 2026-10-31 · Owner instruction · Applies to **future TikTok Affiliate production**, including manual Google Flow prompts and BIMA Production Pack.

## Hard rules (must follow)
1. **Retention-first creative.** The first frame (0.0s) must immediately show a visually compelling, truthful result or product-in-use. **0–2 seconds = scroll-stopping hook.** No generic logo intro, static packaging-only opener, slow empty-frame movement, or long reveal. This is a creative standard, not a guarantee of FYP distribution.
2. **Voice-over is Indonesian by default.** If any narration/VO is included, the **spoken language must be Bahasa Indonesia (id-ID)**, clear and natural with a masculine delivery when appropriate to the user's reference. **No English spoken VO unless the owner explicitly asks for English for that specific content.** A prompt written in English does **not** authorize English narration. Voice-over is optional where the owner asks for music only/no VO.
3. **3 scenes remain the default.** For manually requested Google Flow videos, use **10–15 seconds** (12 seconds as the working template; choose the duration per brief). For the 10-second format: **0–2s hook → 2–6s product detail/proof → 6–10s payoff, natural styling and brief CTA**. Deliver **one combined Google Flow prompt** containing all three scenes when the owner requests one prompt; reference images can be prepared separately per scene. No 3-in-1 photo montage where separate stills are requested.
4. **Preserve reference fidelity.** Keep the user's original model identity, hair, facial proportions and lean/natural body (about 165 cm, 60–61 kg, not bodybuilder), and the real product's exact cut, color, packaging and logo. Do not invent/rewrite branded text. Use realistic Android/UGC camera motion, lighting and contact shadows; avoid visibly artificial motion or beauty smoothing.
5. **Pacing.** Deliver new visual information or a meaningful cut/reframe about every 1–2 seconds, especially early. Show material texture, logo details, fit and final styling rather than long static poses. Conclude with an authentic payoff and optionally a seamless loop. Do not promise that editing alone will force completion.
6. **Audio and commercial integrity.** VO (if requested) must start during the hook, remain succinct enough to fit the actual timeline, and be mixed above **commercially licensed** quiet music. No auto-generated subtitles/on-screen text by default when VO is present unless explicitly requested. No invented product benefits, personal use claims, discount/price or fake yellow-basket overlays. The actual shoppable product attachment remains manual via TikTok until an authorized publishing integration is verified.
7. **Owner approval and data safety.** Do not overwrite prior published/unpublished content, Content Bank records, existing calendars, backups, UI layout or YouTube/Shopee workflows. This standard is applied to **new prompts only**. Nothing here publishes, re-renders or spends credits without authorization.

## Short UGC structure: hook to CTA (owner update 2026-10-31)
Use **one hook → one visible piece of evidence → one payoff → one CTA** per video. Start with the real product in use and keep it visually central; cinematic movement must not obscure the promoted clothing. A new framing every 1–2 seconds can occur within the same three scenes.

| Duration | Scene 1: hook | Scene 2: detail / evidence | Scene 3: payoff + CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10s | 0–2s | 2–6s | 6–10s |
| 12s working template | 0–2s | 2–7s | 7–12s |
| 15s trial | 0–2s | 2–9s | 9–15s |

For 15 seconds, add useful product angles or actions that answer the hook, not longer static poses. Use close-ups of actual texture, cut, logo and fit, followed by a full-body styling payoff. Keep the CTA brief while action continues. Spoken copy must fit a natural Indonesian delivery; shorten it rather than rushing. Reference-video lessons are editorial observations from visible scenes/text, not verified audio transcription or evidence of conversion performance.

CTA examples: “Cek detail dan pilihan ukurannya di keranjang kuning” only when the matching product is actually attached. Otherwise use an accurate neutral CTA, such as “Simpan buat referensi outfit.” Never invent discounts, scarcity, comfort/performance claims or personal experience. Avoid copying a reference creator's script or footage.

## Whittaker trial: 15 distinct content concepts
Owner intends to trial **15 contents**, potentially within Whittaker using different product types. Whittaker is an owner-nominated candidate; do not label any SKU a proven winning product without attributable performance evidence.

- If all 15 use one exact listing/SKU, label the test **single-product creative trial**. If product types/listings differ, label it **Whittaker brand trial** and group results by exact product. Same brand does not mean same product.
- Each content record must identify the actual product name, listing/SKU (null if unavailable), variant/color, reference assets, creative angle, hook, duration, CTA, planned date and actual live URL/date when available. Do not fabricate missing identifiers or create calendar dates without the owner's brief.
- Working matrix: **5 truthful angles × 3 hook approaches = 15 concepts**. Angles: (1) overall fit, (2) visible texture/detail, (3) side/back cut, (4) styling combination, (5) product movement in an appropriate activity. Hook approaches: a specific styling question, a visual detail discovery, and an immediate finished-look reveal. Use only angles supported by the exact product references; replace unsupported angles rather than invent evidence.
- For a clean single-product comparison, vary one main creative element at a time and keep product/variant, duration and CTA comparable. For a brand trial, record product differences as confounders; do not attribute all performance differences to the hook.
- Each video needs a distinct opening, action sequence and useful takeaway; do not simply repost the same video with a different caption. Exact logo, model identity and natural body rules remain mandatory.
- Record organic versus paid traffic separately. Compare at equal post age (for example 24h and 72h), tracking views, average watch time, watch time as a fraction of duration, completion, early drop-off where available, product clicks, orders and attributed commission. Missing metrics stay null. Use repeated results and sample size before choosing a winner; do not guarantee sales or reach.
